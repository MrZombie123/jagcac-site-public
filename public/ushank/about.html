FACTIONS
THE TRASH HEADS
They are conniving bunch of interdimistional mutants and machines.
their army reaches in the billions, just on the nuclear hellscape that they
used to be called earth. with little regard for life, both human and alien,
they will use their overwelming forces to crush any ristance to their rules.
The leaders of the Trash Heads are General Secturary Brushav, and the
elusive D.O.A.W.Y.

THE MINISTRY OF FORCE
The wide (some would call stretch out) collection of the Socianian Republic's
Army, Air Force and Navy. Due to downsizing during the 90s and 2000s they become
a small, albeit very well trained and experience Defence force, and are therefor
to be rekoned with, if you get on their bad side. They're currently looking for
volenteers to help them in the war with the Trash Heads, so sign up at your nearest
local recruitment station today! We Want You!
GOPSTOP RANGERS
a loose cannon group of pirates and thugs involed in the illegal underground
"suger" trade. and also bartering dishwashers and washing machines. they
love drinking partying and most of all, robbery and kidnapping. it is unkown
how small or large their faction is, but it is known that they won't go down
without yelling about how they are on top like a king in a game of draughts.
Their leading figure is only known as Shinless Tom. presumaly a man with no shins.
